# Persist Background Outcomes as Branch-Local Session Entries

Terminal background **Subagent run** outcomes, together with their retained UI-only **Activity tail** (≤50 entries plus the omission count), are stored after the fact as append-only Pi session entries (`customType: "subagent-outcome-record"`, written through the originating session's `pi.appendEntry`) and reconstructed on revisit from the current branch's history (`getBranch()`), so a sibling branch sees only its own results — a receipt without a record reads "outcome unavailable", and nothing on revisit redelivers or starts a new model turn. Hard to reverse once session files contain these entries, and the record is invisible to delivery paths. Surprising — outcomes are usually represented by the **Attributed completion** message, and Pi custom messages already persist on the branch that received them; the record exists so undelivered outcomes and their activity survive too. Trade-off: completeness after revisit vs no crash-proof or exactly-once guarantee — shutdown, replacement, or a crash can prevent the write — and the entry bound is an entry count, not a byte guarantee; the retained activity lives in session storage even though its content never enters model-facing results.
